# Feature Foundation Gates

## Direction gate

Require a clear problem, users, scope, exclusions, risk, options, recommendation, owner-controlled rules, historical behavior, important failures, recovery, and known decision blockers. Direction approval permits drafting only.

## Final artifact gate

Require a complete Foundation, self-review, current content fingerprint, explicit owner approval of that fingerprint, required delegation roles, and no unresolved material decision. Final approval permits specification, not code, installation, deployment, or release.

## Proportionate depth

Use a compact Foundation only for a truly local reversible cosmetic change. Add authoritative rules, worked examples, permission/data controls, reconciliation, recovery, rollout, and specialist checks for Enhanced or Critical changes.
