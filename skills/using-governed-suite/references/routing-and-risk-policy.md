# Routing and risk policy

Use the smallest track that covers the real impact, then escalate for uncertainty. Fast means local and reversible with no behavior, record, permission, calculation, money, compliance, or workflow-state effect. Standard covers ordinary bounded behavior. Enhanced covers security, sensitive data, money, identity, migrations, integrations, bulk actions, multi-tenant boundaries, or declared heavy load. Critical covers irreversible data/production actions, legal or safety effects, or broad blast radius.

The track controls depth of proof; it never makes a missing approval, test, review, or limitation disappear. A host with explicit-only skill routing must be invoked deliberately through `$using-governed-suite` or a project adapter. That is best-effort activation, not automatic enforcement.
