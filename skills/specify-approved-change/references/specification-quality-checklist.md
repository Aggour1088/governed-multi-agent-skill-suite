# Specification Quality Checklist

- Bind the specification to the exact approved Foundation fingerprint.
- Describe observable behavior, not implementation preference.
- State normal, invalid, boundary, permission, failure, interruption, retry, and recovery behavior.
- Define data ownership, audit, historical behavior, calculation examples, and effective dates where relevant.
- Separate owner-controlled policy from protected technical configuration.
- Map every requirement to testable evidence.
- Return any material scope or business-rule change to the Foundation; do not silently clarify it away.
- Do not defer a high-risk behavior to an implementer's judgment.
